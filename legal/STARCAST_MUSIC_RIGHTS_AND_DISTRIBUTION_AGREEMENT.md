## 1. PURPOSE & INTENT

This **Music Distribution, Intellectual Property & Artist Rights Agreement** ("Agreement") establishes the terms under which independent artists, bands, producers, songwriters, and musical acts ("Artist" or "Licensor") upload, distribute, stream, and exhibit sound recordings and musical compositions ("Musical Works") on the StarCast Online broadcast and media platform ("Platform" or "StarCast").

**The core tenets of this Agreement are:**
1. **100% Artist Ownership:** The Artist retains one hundred percent (100%) exclusive ownership of all sound recordings, master rights, lyrics, musical compositions, and publishing rights. StarCast Media claims zero equity, zero copyright, and zero master ownership.
2. **Ad-Free, Non-Exclusive Streaming:** StarCast hosts and streams uploaded Musical Works completely free of commercial third-party advertisements. The Artist grants StarCast solely a non-exclusive, revocable license to transmit the audio to listeners.
3. **Strict Prohibition on Theft & Unauthorized Sampling:** Listeners, third parties, broadcasters, and platform visitors are strictly prohibited from stealing, ripping, sampling without license, re-recording, selling, synching, or commercially exploiting the Artist's Musical Works.
4. **Digital Chain of Custody & Date-Stamped Proof of Ownership:** Every upload generates an immutable, timestamped electronic record ("Certificate of Upload & Chain of Custody") containing track metadata, cryptographic identification, and upload timestamps to serve as verifiable proof of prior-art and ownership in legal enforcement.

## 2. ARTIST COPYRIGHT & 100% OWNERSHIP RETENTION

### 2.1 Complete Reservation of Rights
The Artist retains full, unencumbered, and exclusive copyright in and to all uploaded sound recordings ("Master Recordings") and underlying musical compositions, lyrics, arrangements, and sheet music ("Compositions"). 

### 2.2 Zero Master or Publishing Claim by StarCast
StarCast Media does not acquire, and shall not assert at any time, any ownership, title, copyright transfer, or publishing interest in the Artist's Musical Works. All statutory rights under Title 17 of the United States Code (Copyright Act) and international copyright conventions (Berne Convention) remain solely with the Artist.

### 2.3 Non-Exclusivity
This Agreement is strictly **non-exclusive**. The Artist retains the unrestricted right to distribute, sell, license, perform, monetize, release, and register their Musical Works on any other platform, label, distributor (e.g., Spotify, Apple Music, DistroKid, TuneCore, Bandcamp), radio station, television broadcast, or retail channel.

## 3. GRANT OF NON-EXCLUSIVE BROADCAST LICENSE TO STARCAST

### 3.1 Limited Scope of License
By uploading a Musical Work to StarCast Online, the Artist grants StarCast Media a limited, non-exclusive, royalty-free, worldwide license solely to:
- Host, store, and stream the audio recording on `starcast.online` and the official StarCast Android and web applications;
- Display associated artwork, cover art, track titles, lyrics, and artist biographies;
- Feature the track in StarCast soundstage players, show segments, or editorial playlists;
- Allow platform visitors to stream the audio on-demand without paying fees or viewing third-party banner or video ads.

### 3.2 Optional Artist-Controlled Downloads
If the Artist enables the "Allow Free MP3 Download" toggle in their artist portal, StarCast is authorized to permit personal, non-commercial offline listening downloads. If disabled, direct downloading is forbidden.

### 3.3 Ad-Free Platform Pledge
StarCast Online pledges that Musical Works shall not be interrupted by mid-roll audio ads or commercial pre-roll advertisements. The platform operates as a clean, soundstage-focused music showcase for independent and professional musicians.

### 3.4 Revocation and Deletion
The Artist may remove, unpublish, or delete their Musical Works at any time through the StarCast Artist Portal. Upon deletion, StarCast will cease public streaming within twenty-four (24) hours.

## 4. STRICT PROHIBITION ON MUSIC THEFT, SAMPLING & EXPLOITATION

### 4.1 Prohibition Against Public & Third-Party Infringement
All content streamed on StarCast Online is protected by United States and international copyright laws. Any third party, listener, broadcaster, creator, or entity who accesses Musical Works on the platform is legally bound by the following prohibitions:
- **No Unauthorized Sampling:** No person may sample, chop, loop, isolate vocal/instrumental stems, or incorporate any portion of the Artist's recording or melody into a new song, beat, remix, or recording without an executed commercial licensing agreement signed directly by the Artist.
- **No Re-Recording or "Recording Over":** No person may record vocals, raps, instruments, voiceovers, or commentary over the Artist's tracks or release derivative compositions based upon the Artist's work.
- **No Synchronization or Commercial Placement:** No person may synchronize the Artist's music to film, television, YouTube videos, TikTok/Reels posts, video games, podcasts, or advertisements without the Artist's prior written permission.
- **No Reselling or Redistribution:** No person may rip, scrape, duplicate, sell, upload to streaming DSPs, or redistribute the Artist's audio files.
- **No Artificial Intelligence (AI) Training:** No automated scraper or AI system may ingest, train on, or generate synthetic voices or clones derived from the Artist's audio or lyrics.

## 5. DIGITAL CHAIN OF CUSTODY & DATE-STAMPED PROOF OF OWNERSHIP

### 5.1 Immutable Timestamp Record
At the moment an audio file is uploaded to the StarCast platform, the system ledger automatically records and stores:
1. **Track Title & Track UUID:** Unique system identifier.
2. **Artist / Band Identity:** Registered profile ID and band identifier.
3. **Universal Time (UTC) & Local Server Timestamp:** Exact millisecond date and time of file ingestion.
4. **Original Audio Source URL & Blob Reference:** Storage hash and path.
5. **Electronic Signature / Acknowledgment:** Digital assertion of ownership completed by the uploader.

### 5.2 Certificate of Upload & Legal Evidentiary Use
The Artist may at any time generate, view, download, and print their official **StarCast Certificate of Upload & Chain of Custody Record**. 

In the event that an unauthorized third party copies, samples, steals, or infringes upon the Artist's work, this date-stamped certificate serves as official, non-repudiable evidence of:
- Prior possession and publication date (prior-art);
- Continuous custody of the original master recording;
- Active assertion of copyright ownership dating back to the recorded ingestion timestamp.

### 5.3 DMCA & Infringement Enforcement Assistance
If an Artist discovers that their Musical Work uploaded to StarCast has been stolen or infringed upon by a third party (on YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, social media, or radio), StarCast Media will provide certified server log extracts and written verification of the upload record upon request to support the Artist's DMCA takedown notices, statutory claims, or civil copyright litigation.
